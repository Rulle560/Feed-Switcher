# Attribution and external services

The maintainer confirmed on 2026-10-04 that the project's code, icons and wording may be published under MIT. This statement is recorded with permission from the maintainer; it is not independent proof of a complete provenance audit.

The current extension and local backend are intended to use JavaScript, browser APIs and Node.js built-ins without bundled third-party runtime libraries. Check `package.json` for the actual development tool list. Historical TypeScript sources are retained separately for reference and do not define the current release.

YouTube, Chrome and Cloudflare are external platforms, not project assets or sponsors. Their trademarks and services remain subject to their respective terms. Feed Switcher does not redistribute YouTube media, does not guarantee search ranking, and is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.

The optional Cloudflare Workers/D1 adapter requires a separate self-hosted deployment and service agreement. No public cloud endpoint or paid service is provisioned by this release preparation.

Contributors must identify borrowed code and asset sources and preserve any applicable notices. Do not add third-party media, fonts or libraries without checking their distribution rights.
